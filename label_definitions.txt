analysis: Makes a claim and supports it with a concrete detail or reasoning, such as a number, a timeframe, or a because/therefore.
hot_take: A view about the industry, the market, or AI as a whole, or a question put to the community for its views on such a general topic, offered with no concrete detail or reasoning behind it. The answer matters to everyone in the field, not only to the author.
vent: The point of the post is a feeling (frustration, nostalgia, dread), with little or no argument behind it.
question: Mainly asks for advice, information, or help with the author's own situation (an offer, an interview, a career move), and makes no claim of its own.
